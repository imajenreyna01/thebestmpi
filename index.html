Tujuan Pembelajaran 🎯

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • Identifying the names of school buildings and facilities in English (classrooms, library, laboratory, canteen, etc.).
  • Using prepositions of place (such as next to, across from, between, in front of) to indicate location.
  • Reading and creating a simple map of the school grounds.

Tahukah Kamu?

Untuk menjelaskan di mana letak sebuah ruangan, kita menggunakan Prepositions of Place (Kata depan penunjuk tempat).

  • Next to / Beside: Di sebelah
  • Between: Di antara (2 benda)
  • In front of: Di depan
  • Behind: Di belakang
  • Across from: Di seberang

C. Example Sentences

English Sentence Meaning (Arti)
The library is zznext to the principal's office. Perpustakaan ada di sebelah ruang kepala sekolah.
The canteen is between the clinic and the restroom. Kantin ada di antara klinik dan toilet.
The flag ceremony is in the school yard. Upacara bendera ada di lapangan sekolah.
